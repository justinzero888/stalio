## Full Description

Stalio is a personal habit tracker and journaling app designed to help you build better days — one habit, one note at a time. No accounts, no cloud, no data collection. Everything stays on your device.

**DO — Build Your Day**
Start each morning with a clear view of your habits. Check off daily routines, track streaks, and stay accountable with gentle voice reminders. Whether it's drinking water, meditating, or reading — Stalio keeps your habits front and center.

**TALLY — See Your Progress**
Visualize your journey with beautiful charts and statistics. Track your habit completion streaks, mood patterns, and most-used tags. The Tallies dashboard turns your daily efforts into meaningful insights — so you can see how far you've come.

**TAG — Organize Your Way**
Group your journal entries and habits with custom tags and categories. Create categories like Health, Work, or Learning — and assign tags underneath. Auto-suggest helps you tag entries as you write, and bulk operations make organizing effortless.

**EXPORT — Take Your Data Anywhere**
Your data belongs to you. Export your journal to CSV for spreadsheets or PDF for a beautifully formatted document. Choose a date range and share however you like.

**GROW — One Day at a Time**
Stalio isn't about perfection. It's about showing up, tracking what matters, and watching the small wins compound. No pressure, no social feeds — just you and your journey.

---

### Key Features

- **Habit Tracking** — Daily, weekly, and scheduled habits with streak counting and voice reminders
- **Journaling** — Freeform notes, checklist entries, and emotion tracking with emoji reactions
- **Tag System** — Custom tags organized under named categories with color coding and icons
- **Analytics** — Habit completion charts, mood trends, tag usage statistics, and streak summaries
- **Dark Mode** — Light, dark, and system-follow themes with persistent settings
- **Bilingual** — Full English and Chinese (简体中文) support throughout the app
- **Export** — CSV and PDF export with date range picker — your data, your control
- **Share** — Multi-select notes and share in plain text, Markdown, or styled format
- **Backup & Restore** — Full ZIP backup of all your data; restore anytime

### Privacy First

Stalio does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. No accounts required. No cloud sync. No analytics tracking. All your habits, journal entries, and tags stay stored locally on your device. Your data is yours — period.

### What Stalio Is Not

Stalio is not a social network. There are no followers, likes, or public feeds. It's not an AI coach or chatbot. It's a quiet, focused tool for building a better relationship with your own habits and thoughts.
